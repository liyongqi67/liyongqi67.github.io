Yongqi Li is a Research Assistant Professor in the Department of Computing at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. He has published about 30 papers in top conferences and journals, including ACL/EMNLP, SIGIR/WWW/KDD, ICLR/ICML, CVPR/ACM MM, and TOIS/IP&M. His first author paper was awarded IP&M (CCF B, JCR Q1) Best Ph.D. Paper Award (Honorable Mention). His research interests include natural language processing, information retrieval, and multimodal learning.
Now he mainly focuses on the following research topics:
-NLP (LLM: alignment, reasoning, tool learning, acceleration)
-Multimodal (Multimodal LLM)
-IR (AI search engine, Generative retrieval and recommendation, Web agent)
Our team is looking for highly motivated PhD/master/undergraduate students to collaborate on various interesting research topics, including but not limited to the above ones.
We offer three types of positions:
  •PhD Students (requires a strong background and publications)
  •Research Assistant in Hong Kong (requires a strong background and the completion of relevant research projects)
  •Remote Research Intern (requires good coding skills, with the goal of publishing papers)
